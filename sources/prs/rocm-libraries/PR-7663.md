## Description

> PyTorch started dispatching large convolutions to MIOpen as of pytorch 2.11/2.12, but the MIOpen support is limited and requires additional work on the MIOpen side to fully support the large convolutions. In particular, the six grouped CK xdlops solvers (`ConvHipImplicitGemmGroup{Fwd,Bwd,Wrw}Xdlops` and their 3D counterparts) are marked inapplicable for convolutions whose tensor element-strides exceed the int32 range, such as `(1,96,4736,4736)` or `(1,96,1024,1024,1024)`.
